Breaking: Tech Layoffs Hit 50,000 Workers This Week

TechPulse News • November 4, 2026

A wave of layoffs swept through the technology sector this week as major companies slashed tens of thousands of roles amid declining ad revenue and a prolonged period of economic uncertainty.

Executives cited over-hiring during the pandemic boom, rising interest rates, and pressure from shareholders to cut costs as primary drivers behind the decisions.

Workers in engineering, sales, and operations were disproportionately affected. Some employees received termination notices with as little as 30 minutes of advance warning before losing access to company systems.

Employee advocacy groups have called for greater transparency and severance packages that reflect the contributions made during the hyper-growth years.
